## Design principles

- Raw observations are immutable snapshots; never overwrite a historical value.
- Derived indicator signals are separate from source observations.
- Weekly scores and probabilities are model outputs, not source facts.
- Every observation should preserve provenance and confidence.
- Forecasts should include a resolution date or condition whenever possible.
## Core indicator families

- Accelerator demand and semiconductor supply
- Hyperscaler AI capex and guidance
- GPU rental pricing and utilization proxies
- Frontier-model revenue and economics
- Enterprise AI adoption and ROI
- Neocloud and data-center credit conditions
- Power, construction and interconnection constraints
- Semiconductor inventory and lead times
- Private AI valuations and funding
- Project cancellations, delays and restructurings
## Scoring

The weekly Bubble Risk Score is 0–100. Higher values indicate greater risk that AI infrastructure investment and financing materially correct. The 18–24 month burst probability is stored separately so changes to the scoring model remain auditable.
